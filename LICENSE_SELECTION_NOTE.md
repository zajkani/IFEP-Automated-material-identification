# License selection note — do not publish this as the repository's LICENSE

Before adding a `LICENSE` file, decide what permissions you intend to grant.

- MIT is a common permissive license and permits commercial reuse subject to its terms.
- BSD-3-Clause is also permissive and includes a non-endorsement condition.
- GPL-3.0 imposes conditions on redistribution of covered derivative works.
- If you are not ready to grant reuse rights, do not add an open-source license yet; public visibility alone does not grant broad reuse permission.

Confirm ownership and permissions for all included code, contributions, data, and figures. Check university/employer, co-author, funding, and publisher requirements. This note is not legal advice.

Once you choose, replace this note with the full official license text and update `license` in `CITATION.cff`.
